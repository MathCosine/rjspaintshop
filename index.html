Inside the Finish

Knowledgeable about every layer.

A factory finish is a system, not a single coat of paint. We rebuild it layer by layer, so the repair matches the rest of your car.

Our Story

20+ Years of
Uncompromising Quality

RJ's Paint Shop has been Pleasanton's trusted name in auto body and paint since the early 2000s. We built our reputation on meticulous craftsmanship, honest service, and results that speak for themselves.

As Tesla repair specialists, we're equipped with the training, tools, and materials to handle EV-specific body work. After years of caring for BMWs, Porsches, and other high-end cars, we now focus solely on Tesla. Every car that leaves our shop meets factory standards — no exceptions.
